=== free tier arithmetic, over GitHub's published rates ===
a 2 core Codespace bills 2 core hours per wall clock hour
the personal free allowance is 120 core hours a month, so 60 hours on a 2 core machine
at one lesson a day for thirty days that is 2 hours a day before a reader pays

storage is billed separately against a 15 GB month allowance, and the image is on the disk:
  unpacked image 1.7G, against a 32 GB disk on the smallest machine
  so a codespace left alive for a month spends roughly a sixth of the storage allowance
  and a reader who deletes it between sessions spends almost none
